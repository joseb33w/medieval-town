# Goal
A small walkable medieval town ("Ashford Green") as a data-driven Godot 4.7.1 game (rpg streaming
template, chunk mode, native-playable `world.json`). The player spawns on the square, can open the
town hall's front door with USE, walk in, climb a real stair to a real second floor, and look out
through windows that are true openings in the walls. Regular houses (with real window holes and
hollow interiors), a tavern, a chapel, market stalls, fields and orchards surround it.

# Files to touch
- `world.json` — generated by `tools/build_town.py` from `tools/world_layout.json` (Architect
  specialist's macro layout) + `tools/gameplay.json` (sky, director, regions, zones, rules).
- `quests.json` — two-stage director chain (talk to the steward, reach the council chamber; meet the townsfolk).
- `structures.json` — Mason B-rep specs for the enterable buildings (Structures specialist); compiled to
  `models/<type>.glb` (real openings, stairs) and post-processed by `tools/pp.mjs`.
- `models/*.glb` — Mason buildings + Meshy rigged cast (player, steward, blacksmith, market woman, guard, villager).
- `audio/*.ogg` — calm_town music, forest_birds / town_crowd / campfire ambient beds (CC0 library).
- No engine `.gd` changes (the template stays byte-identical so the game stays native-playable).

# Verification approach
- Mason walk gate on every compiled building; `qgcheck` winnability on `world.json`+`quests.json`.
- Headless export (nothreads, gl_compatibility) + the canonical `verify.mjs` smoke/feel pass.
- Targeted checks: door leaves land in the doorways (geometry check from the compiled spec), the
  upper-floor zone fires the `hall_upper` flag, stairs are walkable (Mason ray-cast gate).
- Independent QA + Game-Feel specialist reviews on the content-complete export.

# Out of scope
- Combat, enemies, multiplayer, backend / accounts (single-player, no persistence needed).
- Furnished interiors beyond a few props; per-house NPC schedules.
