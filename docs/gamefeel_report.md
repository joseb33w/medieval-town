## ❗ P1 — must fix

### P1-1  "The door swings open." is a persistent panel that follows you through the whole hall, and "tap dialogue" does NOT dismiss it
- **Symptom.** USE on the hall door opens it and pops a dark panel at the top ("The door swings open.") plus a yellow "tap dialogue / USE to continue" line drawn across the hero's chest. It never times out: still up 5 s later at the door (`port2-06-door-after-use-5s.png`), inside the hall (`port2-07-inside-hall.png`, `land2-03-inside-hall.png`), at the stair (`land3-01-stair-approach.png`), on the upper floor and at the council-chamber window (`land4-01…04-*.png`). While it is up the USE prompt for everything else is replaced by "tap dialogue / USE to continue", and the next USE only dismisses it — a routine door costs two taps.
- **Tapping the panel does nothing.** A touch on the panel text (`port3-02-dismiss-A-tap-text.png`) and on the panel's lower body (`port3-03-dismiss-B-tap-panel-body.png`) both left it up; only the USE button cleared it (`port3-04-dismiss-C-use.png`). So the on-screen instruction is wrong and the only way out is a button the player is told is optional.
- **HUD collisions while it is up.** The panel occupies HUD y 40–150 and the quest label starts at y 140: the panel's bottom edge sits on the "QUEST:" line in both aspects (`port2-05-door-after-use.png`, `land2-02-door-opened.png`). In landscape the hall toast (y 16 %) is then drawn straight across the panel's bottom edge AND the quest label's first line — three text layers stacked (`land2-03-inside-hall.png`).
- **Root cause (ENGINE, `interaction.gd`).** `_open_door()` → `_show(["The door swings open."])` (l.513); `_advance()` only runs from `try_use()` or the panel's `gui_input` (l.874). The panel's child `dlg_label` is a `RichTextLabel` (l.876–881) inside a `PanelContainer`, so it is stretched to the full panel and keeps the default `MOUSE_FILTER_STOP` — it swallows every touch, and the container's `gui_input` never fires. No timeout exists. The old `quiet_door_*` data trick (pre-open via `interact`→`open_door`) is dead under the new toggle engine: `_open_door` on an already-open door now closes it and shows "The door swings shut." (l.497–500).
- **Engine fix direction.** Make system notices non-modal: auto-clear `dlg_box` ~2 s after `_show` for door/chest lines (or route them through `shell._toast`), and set `dlg_label.mouse_filter = Control.MOUSE_FILTER_IGNORE` so the panel's own tap handler is reachable. Keep the modal path only for the locked-door message.
- **DATA mitigation (VERIFIED in `/tmp/gf/out2`, world.json only).** Open the door *for* the visitor as they arrive, so USE is never needed on the critical path — this also matches the steward's line "the hall door is open to visitors":
  ```json
  "zones": [ …, {"name": "hall_porch", "center": [10, 1.0, 18.0], "size": [8.0, 3.0, 5.0]} ],
  "rules": [ {"id": "hall_door_porch", "when": {"event": "enter_zone", "target": "hall_porch"},
              "then": [{"open_door": "door_town_hall_0_0_0"}]}, … ]
  ```
  Console: `GOGI_RULE_FIRED hall_door_porch on=enter_zone`; the leaf swings as the player reaches the porch, the prompt reads "USE > Close Town Hall Door" (`fix-01-door-approach.png`), and the interior HUD is clean — quest label + transient toast only (`fix-02-inside-no-panel.png`). **Do not** use a `start` rule for this: at `start` the cell has not built yet and it logs `GOGI_RULES_UNIMPL open_door:no door named 'door_town_hall_0_0_0'` (observed). Reword quest step 2 ("Go into the hall and climb the stairs…") and the title hint if the door no longer needs opening. A curious USE on the open door still closes it and shows the "swings shut" panel — the engine fix above is still needed for that path.

### P1-2  The camera leaves the building: at the upper-floor front wall the frame is 100 % wall with NO hero — exactly where the quest sends you
- **Symptom.** On the council-chamber floor, walking toward the front wall/windows (the quest's final beat): portrait at (8.3, 3.6, 13.65) → the whole frame is beige wall, hero invisible, exterior window slots visible at the top edge (`port3-11-upper-front-wall.png`); landscape at (12.2, 3.6, 13.5) → same (`land4-02-upper-near-window.png`). At the stair top (5.6, 3.6, 13.6) the camera is outside looking at the façade and the open door below, hero hidden (`port3-09-stair-top.png`). At the stair approach on the ground floor the camera is outside the west/front wall watching the hero through a window slot (`port3-07-stair-approach.png`, `land3-01-stair-approach.png`); mid-stair it sits inside the front wall's thickness (`land3-02-mid-stair.png`, `port3-08-mid-stair.png`). Standing 1 m in front of a house façade with the camera on the wall side also blanks the hero (near-cull) and shows only the lane (`port4-12-house-door-prompt.png`).
- **Root cause (ENGINE, `main.gd:2978-2988`).** `SpringArm3D` with no shape (a ray), `spring_length 8.5`, `margin 0.3`, default `cam_pitch −0.55`; the landmark collider is the exact render trimesh (`collider: "mesh_exact"`, no `__collision` shell in `town_hall.glb`). A ray slips through the stairwell cutout and the 0.55–0.7 m window/arch openings, and once the hit point is outside the building the camera is placed outside; against the front wall it either passes an arch or collapses to <1.35 m and the avatar is hidden. No data hook exists for camera distance/pitch/shape.
- **Fix direction (engine).** Give the arm a `SphereShape3D` (r ≈ 0.35) so it cannot thread a 0.7 m arch or the stairwell gap; add an indoor preset (short upward probe hits a ceiling → `spring_length` ~4.5, pitch ~−0.25); when the arm collapses below the avatar-hide threshold, prefer pulling the pitch up rather than hiding the hero. **Data mitigation (not verified, closest available):** thin `rows`/`part` boxes with `collider: "box"` in each hall opening (e.g. `material: "glass"`) would stop the ray while reading as glazing — at the cost of the "real open window" look; ~20 openings on the hall alone.

### P1-3  Indoors the camera collapses onto the hero: 35–55 % of the portrait frame is character, the sword crosses the SHEATHE/USE buttons
- **Symptom.** Every interior frame at the default pitch: ground-floor centre (`port3-06-hall-centre.png`, `port5-04-gf-centre.png`, `port5-05-gf-centre-orbit.png` — hero ≈55 % of frame height, sword drawn over SHEATHE), upper floor centre top-down (`port3-10-upper-centre.png`), stair top (`port3-08-mid-stair.png` — hero 50 %, sword to the bottom edge). Orbiting in the 4 m lane beside the hall does the same (`port4-11-alley-orbit.png` — hero 45 %, red house wall 50 %). Landscape is milder (hero ≈25 %, `land2-03-inside-hall.png`) because the wider frame keeps the arm longer.
- **Root cause (ENGINE constants).** `CAM_DIST 8.5` at `cam_pitch −0.55` puts the camera 4.4 m above the head; every 3.4 m-high storey slab stops the arm at ≈3 m, and portrait's tall FOV magnifies the result. Same family as P1-2 but a different fix: this is the *pitch/ceiling* collapse, not ray slip.
- **Fix direction (engine).** Indoor preset as above (shorter arm + shallower pitch under a ceiling); or clamp pitch to ≈−0.3 while a ceiling probe is positive. No data lever; the `hall_ground`/`hall_upper` zones already exist and would be the natural trigger if the engine exposed a `camera` rule action.

## ✅ Passed

- **Touch controls, one-handed, end to end (P0 class clear), both aspects.** Invisible left-half joystick: z 30→24.9 on a 1.5 s hold (portrait), 30→23.45 on 3 s (landscape). Right-half drag orbits: `cam_yaw` 0→1.08 (portrait) / 0→1.39 (landscape), reverse drag restores 0. USE button opens the door (`port2-05-door-after-use.png`), talks to the steward (`GOGI_RULE_FIRED steward_met on=talk`), dismisses the panel; JUMP button: y 0→1.68, `anim: jump`, lands (`step8_jump` trace); SHEATHE toggles to DRAW. Button grid portrait 85×72 css px cells at x ≥ 305 / y ≥ 591 px; landscape 128×52 px at x ≥ 584 / y ≥ 192 px — all inside the right half, none overlapping, none off-screen; `_touch_on_hud` keeps button presses from starting an orbit.
- **HUD fits a phone**: no overflow at either aspect; quest label, toast, prompt and buttons do not collide *except* in the P1-1 door-panel state. **No debug text ships** in gameplay (grid/VRAM/rule lines are console-only; `hud_debug` off).
- **Title screen**: "Ashford / Green" clean in both aspects, tagline explains the invisible joystick/drag/USE, single "ENTER THE TOWN" button in the centre band is tappable (portrait ≈0.59–0.63 h, landscape ≈0.70 h), the "Tap to play" audio gate is one tap.
- **Transient vs persistent**: region name "Market Square" is a fading toast, never pinned; hall / council-chamber / steward toasts fade (gone by `port2-09-inside-orbit.png`); the quest label is the only pinned text and it is the objective. No stale status text.
- **Door toggles** ("USE > Close Town Hall Door" once open, `port3-04-dismiss-C-use.png`), leaf sized to the doorway, prompt range (~2.9 m from the hinge) feels right; the steward is 4.1 m off the hinge so the door wins from the corridor centre.
- **First-run orientation**: the hall is dead ahead 15 m with the steward beside its door and lamps flanking it (`land1-05-door-prompt.png`, `land1-03-spawn-settled.png`); the quest text says exactly what to do; the toast inside points to the correct (back-left) stair; stairs climb cleanly to the upper floor (`GOGI_RULE_FIRED upstairs on=enter_zone`).
- **Camera in the open**: alleys frame well when the camera trails the hero (`port4-10-alley.png`), the square and High Street read as a town (`land1-04-orbit.png`); orbit never pitches into floor/sky beyond the clamped range.
- **Damage feedback**: no combat, no modal banners anywhere; `shake` is the only hit action available.
- **Windows are real openings** — narrow (0.55–0.7 m) mason slots; from inside you see the square through them when the camera stays inside (`port3-12-upper-look-out.png`, `land4-03-upper-look-out.png`).
- No script errors in any run (the only console errors are the sandbox's blocked TTS fetches).
