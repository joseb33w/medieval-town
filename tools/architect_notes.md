# Ashford Green — architect notes (macro layout for `world.json`)

Chunk mode, `cell_size` 20, 7×7 grid (gx, gz ∈ −3..3, all 49 cells present, contiguous), flat ground, no
`terrain`/`water`/`roads`. `start_cell` [0,1], `goal` reach_cell [0,0]. Generated by `/tmp/gen_ashford.py`
(copy in `/mnt/session/outputs/gen_ashford.py`) — re-run it to regenerate after tweaks; it also runs a
geometry check (Mason footprint overlaps, footprint-vs-lane, footprint-vs-spawn, props inside footprints,
props on lanes, manifest id existence, ≤3 Mason per cell, Mason first in `props`, ≤12 props, ≤40 scatter).

## Compass / conventions
- World centre of cell [gx,gz] = (gx·20+10, 0, gz·20+10). Every `pos` is cell-local [x,z] from that centre.
- **−Z = NORTH, +Z = SOUTH, +X = EAST, −X = WEST.**
- Mason `rot` 0 → door faces **north (−Z)**; 180 → **south (+Z)**; 90 → **west (−X)**; −90 → **east (+X)**.
- Door centre = building pos + rotate((door_offset, −depth/2)) — table below is computed by the script.

## Deviations / things the coordinator should know
1. **Lane strips**: I used `size [4,0.06,3.5]` with `spacing 4` from −8 to 8 (instead of 3.5/3.4) so the
   boxes tile edge-to-edge — the 3.5/3.4 recipe overlaps coplanar tops and z-fights. At T/cross junctions the
   perpendicular half-row skips the centre box for the same reason (a ~0.25 m ground seam at the junction).
2. **`"cobble"` is not a GSurf part material** (only a ground preset) → lane parts use an explicit surface
   dict `{"color":[0.42,0.41,0.43],"rough":0.8,"bump":0.65,"tile":1.2}`; outskirts paths use the `"dirt"` preset.
3. **`Stone_Arch` is NOT used**: measured it — a 1×1.4×2 m arch-TOP module floating at y 1.6–3.0 (needs
   pillars); grounded alone it is a 1.4 m block. Gates are instead a 1 m `Stone_Wall_1` row with a 6 m gap
   flanked by two lamps.
4. **Scale on props** (delegation said omit; I used it deliberately after measuring): Quaternius trees are
   2.5–3.5 m at 1.0, so tree PROPS carry `scale 2.2` (5.5–7.5 m); `Prop_Lamp_Street` is 1.25 m → `scale 2.0`;
   `Canopy_Full` is a 1.2 m kit module → `scale 2.5`. Strip these if you disagree. Row parts and scatter can't
   take a scale, so orchard rows / forest scatter trees stay small (reads as fruit trees / undergrowth).
5. **Module sizes measured**: Stone_Wall_1 = 1.0 m along Z (rows E–W use `rot 90`, spacing 1.0);
   Prop_WoodenFence_Single = 2.06 m along X (N–S rows use `rot 90`); farm `Fence` = 5.9 m; Bench 2.78 m along X;
   Windmill 6.5×1.9×11.2 m; SmallBarn ≈6×6.3; OpenBarn ≈5.7×6.2; Prop_Wagon 4 m along Z; carts 3.4 m along Z.
6. **`rings` are always centred on the cell centre and use one fixed rot**, so they can't make a rectangular
   plank fence (two sides would be perpendicular). The three pastures use a ring of timber posts
   (`column r0.12 h1.0`); real fences are `rows` per side, with gaps left as gates so no cell is sealed.
7. **Scatter is random over the whole cell** (engine): in house cells I only scatter short `Grass`/`Flowers`
   (count 8–14); a few stalks may poke through a Mason floor. Cobble cells [0,0] and [0,1] carry NO scatter
   (grass on cobble reads wrong and the hall interior is the showcase). If the runtime ever masks scatter under
   footprints, counts can go up.
8. **`populate` spawns at random cell points**; I kept crowds out of [0,0] (the hall covers a third of it) —
   the plaza crowd lives in [0,1] instead.
9. Tavern chest at [2,1] local (−3.5, −6.5) is inside the tavern's west end; if the Mason interior puts
   furniture there, nudge it.
10. Live node budget: heaviest cells are wheat fields (~80 wheat row nodes, collider none) and the chapel yard
    (≈57 wall modules); every cell ≤12 props, scatter ≤40/entry, rows ≤ 25 parts each.
